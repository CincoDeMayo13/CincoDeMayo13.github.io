Our Solutions

Physical / Digital ID

In the field of Citizen Identity Verification, we launched a series of products and solutions designed to provide strong support for government identity management systems. We issue secure identity documents to enhance the authentication and protection of personal identity.

Enrolment / Issuance

Enrolment and credential issuance methods have evolved. Identity proofing is performed across multiple touch points like manned counters, mobile apps and websites. Credentials are issued in physical and virtual form factors.

Card Production and Fulfilment Services

Offering high-quality, customised payment cards is essential to delivering a superior customer experience. Our Card Production and Fulfilment Service provides a comprehensive solution for manufacturing and fulfilling anonymous and personalised EMV (contactless) cards, ensuring that your cardholders receive professional, secure, and fully compliant products.

Consultancy

We design, implement and manage identity programs across physical and digital boundaries, addressing complex use cases such as Fraud Prevention and Smart Access Control.
